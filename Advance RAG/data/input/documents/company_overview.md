# TechNova Corporation: Company Overview

TechNova Corporation is a fictional enterprise software company founded
in 2018 and headquartered in Austin, Texas. Its mission is to make secure
automation understandable and accessible to mid-sized organizations.
The company serves 1,240 active customers in North America and Europe.

## Leadership and departments

Maya Chen is Chief Executive Officer. Omar Singh leads Engineering,
Elena Rossi leads Customer Success, Jordan Blake leads Security, Priya
Nair leads People Operations, Avery Morgan leads Sales and Marketing,
and Mateo Alvarez leads Finance and Administration as Chief Financial
Officer. The six operating departments are Engineering, Customer
Success, Security, Sales and Marketing, People Operations, and Finance
and Administration. The fictional company uses a July-to-June fiscal year.

TechNova's three products are NovaDesk, NovaFlow, and NovaSecure. Product
facts elsewhere in this repository take precedence over general
marketing descriptions.
