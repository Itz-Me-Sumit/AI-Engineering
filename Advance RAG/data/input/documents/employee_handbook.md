# TechNova Employee Handbook (Effective July 1, 2025)

## Work model
Employees may work remotely within their approved country. Teams hold
collaboration hours from 10:00 to 15:00 Central Time, Tuesday through
Thursday. Moving to another country requires People Operations approval.

## Paid time off
Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual paid time off, 10 company
holidays, and 5 paid wellness days. PTO requests of five consecutive
workdays or more should be submitted 30 calendar days in advance.
Unused PTO carries over up to 5 days; wellness days do not carry over.

## Learning and equipment
Each employee receives a USD 1,500 annual learning budget. Manager
approval is required for a single purchase over USD 500. The home-office
allowance is USD 800 once every three years. TechNova-owned laptops must
be returned within 10 calendar days after employment ends.

## Conduct
Confidential customer data may only be stored in approved systems.
Suspected harassment, retaliation, or conflicts of interest can be
reported to a manager, People Operations, or the anonymous ethics line.
