# TechNova FY2025 Annual Report (Fictional)

For the fiscal year ended June 30, 2025, TechNova recorded revenue of
$48.6 million, up 35% from $36.0 million in FY2024. Subscription revenue
was $43.2 million and professional services revenue was $5.4 million.
Gross margin was 74%. Operating expenses were $38.7 million and net loss
was $2.8 million. Cash at year end was $21.4 million.

Product revenue mix was NovaDesk 46%, NovaFlow 34%, and NovaSecure 20%.
Customer count rose from 930 to 1,180 by fiscal year end, while gross
revenue retention was 94%. No single customer represented more than 6%
of revenue.

Headcount at year end was 286: Engineering 112, Customer Success 68,
Security 34, Sales and Marketing 42, People Operations 12, and Finance
and Administration 18. FY2026 priorities are EU resilience, faster
NovaFlow execution, and expansion of NovaSecure evidence automation.
