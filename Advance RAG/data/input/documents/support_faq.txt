TECHNOVA SUPPORT FAQ — January 2026

Standard support hours are Monday-Friday, 08:00-18:00 Central Time,
excluding company holidays. Premium support is available 24x7.

Severity 1 means a production service is unavailable for most users with
no workaround. Initial response targets are 30 minutes for Premium and
2 hours for Standard. Severity 2 targets are 2 hours for Premium and
8 business hours for Standard.

Customers can reset an administrator password from Settings > Identity
when at least one other administrator is active. If all administrators
are locked out, an authorized account contact must open a support case.

NovaDesk exports are retained for 7 days. Deleted tickets remain in the
recovery area for 30 days. NovaFlow failed runs can be replayed for 14
days. Status notices are posted at status.technova.example.
