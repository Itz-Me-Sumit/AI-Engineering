# Products and Pricing (USD, effective January 2026)

## NovaDesk
Customer support workspace with knowledge search and ticket summaries.
Starter costs $29 per agent/month with a 5-agent minimum. Business costs
$59 per agent/month with a 10-agent minimum and includes SSO. Enterprise
pricing is custom and includes regional data residency.

## NovaFlow
Workflow automation for approvals and operational tasks. Team costs
$399 per workspace/month and includes 20 active workflows. Scale costs
$899 per workspace/month and includes 100 active workflows. Additional
workflow packs of 25 cost $120/month.

## NovaSecure
Security posture and access-review platform. Standard costs $1,200 per
organization/month for up to 500 employees. Advanced costs $2,400 per
organization/month for up to 2,000 employees and includes automated
evidence collection.

Annual prepaid subscriptions receive a 12% discount. Discounts do not
stack. Prices exclude tax, implementation, and premium support.
