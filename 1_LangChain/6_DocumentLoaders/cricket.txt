1. Cricket sirf ek khel nahi, ek jazbaat hai,

2. Har fan ke dil ki ye khaas si baat hai.

3. Bat haath mein ho, toh sapne jagte hain,

4. Maidan mein utarte hi hausle badhte hain.

5. Pitch par jab ball pehli baar aati hai,

6. Har aankh bas us pal ko takti jaati hai.

7. Bowler daudta hai poore josh ke saath,

8. Batsman khada rehta hai jeet ki aas.

9. Ek chhoti si ball, ek bada sa imtihaan,

10. Har delivery mein chhupa hota hai armaan.

11. Kabhi cover drive dil ko chhoo jaata hai,

12. Kabhi yorker stumps ko uda jaata hai.

13. Kabhi chhakka aasman ko choo leta hai,

14. Kabhi catch haathon se chhoot jaata hai.

15. Kabhi jeet ki khushi aankhon mein hoti hai,

16. Kabhi haar ki nami palkon mein hoti hai.

17. Par asli khiladi kabhi rukta nahi,

18. Girkar bhi woh kabhi jhukta nahi.

19. Har haar use kuch sikha jaati hai,

20. Har jeet nayi umeed jagati hai.

21. Galiyon se lekar bade stadium tak,

22. Cricket ka junoon chalta hai har kadam tak.

23. Bachcha ho ya bada, sabka ek khwab,

24. Haath mein bat ho aur saamne ho lajawab jawab.

25. Koi Sachin banne ka sapna dekhta hai,

26. Koi Dhoni jaisa finisher banna chahta hai.

27. Koi Kohli ki tarah run banana chahe,

28. Koi Bumrah ki tarah wickets lekar aaye.

29. Koi spin se batsman ko nachata hai,

30. Koi speed se balle ko hilaata hai.

31. Stadium mein jab crowd chillata hai,

32. Har player ka hausla badh jaata hai.

33. "Jeetega bhai jeetega" jab awaaz aaye,

34. Khiladi apna sab kuch maidan mein lagaye.

35. Tiranga jab hawa mein lehraata hai,

36. Har dil deshbhakti se bhar jaata hai.

37. Jersey pe naam chahe kisi ka ho,

38. Dil mein desh ka hi naam hota hai.

39. Ek boundary par hazaar dil dhadakte hain,

40. Ek wicket par lakhon haath uthte hain.

41. Kabhi match aakhri over tak jaata hai,

42. Har ball par naya suspense ban jaata hai.

43. Chaar run chahiye, chhe ball baaki,

44. Har nazar scoreboard par hoti hai taaki.

45. Bowler ke haath mein aakhri mauka,

46. Batsman ke dil mein jeet ka dhoka.

47. Ball hawa mein jaati hai door,

48. Fielder bhaagta hai poore zor.

49. Catch pakda ya boundary gayi,

50. Ek pal mein kahani badal gayi.

51. Cricket humein patience sikhata hai,

52. Har mushkil mein balance banana batata hai.

53. Har ball ko naya mauka samajhna hai,

54. Har setback ke baad phir se ladna hai.

55. Kabhi defense bhi zaroori hota hai,

56. Har waqt attack karna zaroori nahi hota hai.

57. Kabhi single se innings banti hai,

58. Kabhi ek chhakke se duniya hasti hai.

59. Teamwork iska sabse bada raaz hai,

60. Milkar khelna hi asli andaaz hai.

61. Wicketkeeper peeche se awaaz lagata hai,

62. Bowler apna plan samjhata hai.

63. Fielder boundary par taiyaar khada,

64. Captain har pal rakhta hai nazar bada.

65. Ek team jab saath mein khelti hai,

66. Tabhi jeet ki kahani likhti hai.

67. Koi star ho ya ho naya khiladi,

68. Team ke liye sabki hoti hai zimmedari.

69. Cricket mein ego ki jagah nahi,

70. Team se badi koi baat nahi.

71. Kabhi baarish match ko rok deti hai,

72. Kabhi dhoop himmat ko aazma leti hai.

73. Kabhi pitch slow, kabhi fast hoti hai,

74. Har condition mein strategy khaas hoti hai.

75. Kabhi bat chamakta hai roshni mein,

76. Kabhi ball ghoomti hai hawa mein.

77. Har over ek nayi kahani kehta hai,

78. Har innings ek naya sapna deta hai.

79. Score chahe kam ho ya zyada,

80. Jab tak match khatam nahi, tab tak koi haara nahi.

81. Cricket bachpan ki yaadon ka naam hai,

82. Doston ke saath guzra har shaam hai.

83. Gali mein bana chhota sa stadium,

84. Eent ka wicket aur sapnon ka aasman.

85. Out hone par behas bhi hoti thi,

86. Jeetne par khushi bhi hoti thi.

87. Na helmet tha, na proper bat,

88. Phir bhi cricket mein tha alag hi swag.

89. Woh bachpan ka junoon aaj bhi zinda hai,

90. Har cricket lover ke dil mein basta hai.

91. Cricket humein sapne dekhna sikhata hai,

92. Aur un sapnon ke liye ladna sikhata hai.

93. Haar aaye toh himmat na haar,

94. Jeet mile toh na karna ahankaar.

95. Maidan mein hamesha imaandari rakhna,

96. Apni team ke liye poori jaan laga dena.

97. Cricket ka asli matlab yahi toh hai,

98. Junoon, mehnat aur dosti ka mel yahi toh hai.

99. Bat, ball aur dil jab saath aate hain,

100. Tab cricket keval khel nahi, yaadein ban jaate hain.
